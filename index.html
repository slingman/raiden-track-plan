Where this actually happens: a parking lot, not a track — 50m measured out with a wheel ruler, mostly flat concrete, hand-timed with a stopwatch. That's fine for this plan, with a few adjustments:
  • Regular cushioned running shoes are the call — no spikes needed, which matches what the coaches want for an athlete this age anyway, even on an actual track. Currently running in Brooks Anthem 7s.
  • Concrete has zero give compared to a track, so it loads joints harder — take the "pain signals" note below extra seriously, and do plyo work (pogo hops, bounds, box step-ups) on grass or a mat if one's available.
  • Hand-timed splits run ~0.2-0.3s slower than blocks + laser timing (reaction lag on both ends). Currently averaging ~8.45s over the measured 50m — treat that as a consistent baseline to track week-over-week improvement, not a literal 50m time. Goal: ~0.3-0.6s faster (~7.85-8.15s) by the end of Phase 3 in May — a realistic 4-7% gain for a first structured season, most of it landing once Phase 2's start/acceleration work kicks in.
  • No blocks needed — standing/2-point starts (already in the plan) are the right call for this setup.
  • Rule of thumb: sub-max technique work, light plyo, and strength/gym sessions are fine on the lot. Near-max or max-effort sprints, flying sprints, and heavier plyo volume (mostly Phase 2 onward) do better with track's surface give — sessions below are tagged Lot ok / Track preferred accordingly.
Fitting it around school + dinner: school lets out at 3:30pm Mon/Tue/Thu and 2:30pm Wed/Fri, and dinner's ~6-6:30pm every night — that leaves a comfortable window on every track day with room to spare for warm-up, the session, and cooldown before eating.

Managing three activities at once

Total load

Aim for at most 4-5 genuinely hard days a week across boxing, tennis, and track combined — not per activity.

Recovery

Sleep is the real recovery tool. 9+ hours matters more than anything done in a session.

Pain signals

Sharp pain — especially knees, shins, heels, growth-plate areas — is a stop-and-check signal, not push-through.

Priority

Let one thing lead each season. Track workouts get the earlier, fresher slots once season starts.

What actually moves a 50m time

Most of this plan is built around the first two — that's where a 13-year-old has the most room to improve fast.

Reaction + first 3 steps off the line

Pure technique, practiced constantly through the whole season.

Drive phase, staying low 0-20m

Technique plus a bit of strength — this is Phase 2's centerpiece.

Transition to upright sprinting ~20-30m

Where drive phase hands off to full stride.

Relaxed top-end speed 30-50m

Kids often tense up here and slow down — relaxation is the skill, not more effort.
